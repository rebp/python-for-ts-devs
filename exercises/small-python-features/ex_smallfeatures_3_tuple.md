# Exercise 4.4-3: Read the small syntax

Predict the types of `(1)` and `(1,)`, and unpack `[1, 2, 3, 4]` into a
first value, a middle list and a last value. Then choose between `NamedTuple`,
a dataclass and `TypedDict` for an immutable point, a mutable record and a
JSON-shaped dictionary.

Hint: the comma, not the parentheses, makes a tuple.
